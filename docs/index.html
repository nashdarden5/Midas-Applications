Quick workflow

1. Swipe through GovDeals and GSA vehicle listings above. 2. Tap 📌 Save to BlakeyPoo on any vehicle, no copying needed. 3. Open Saved to revisit the listing and track it. 4. Tap its green star in the carousel to inspect estimated resale profit and linked sold comparisons where available.

For GovDeals auctions, open the official website and use Add to save its URL manually. One-tap saving currently works on the GSA carousel.

📲 Install & share

iPhone: Open this page in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Your approved BlakeyPoo icon will appear on the shortcut.

Android: Open in Chrome → menu → Add to Home screen or Install app.

No IPA, Sideloadly, or Apple signing. Auction shortcuts and estimates are stored locally and won't automatically sync to friends' devices.

BlakeyPoo is independent, not affiliated with GovDeals. Auction listings, sign-in, bids and purchases take place on the official GovDeals website. Estimated upside is not guaranteed profit.
* Estimated upside = resale estimate − purchase estimate − fees/shipping. Excludes taxes, labor, returns, and unentered costs. No live auction feed or automatic bidding.
